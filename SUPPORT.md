# 支持 / Support

## 获取帮助

- 使用方法、算法和边界条件请先查阅软件内置技术手册及 [工程文档](docs/README.md)。
- 可复现的软件缺陷请使用 GitHub 的 Bug report。
- 小范围兼容性、文档或维护建议请使用 Maintenance proposal。
- 安全问题请遵循 [安全策略](SECURITY.md)，不要创建公开 Issue。

本项目不提供商业支持、私有格式逆向、传感器调参服务或通用 ISP 方案。维护者会尽力处理清晰且符合范围的问题，但不承诺响应或修复时限。

请勿公开上传客户 RAW、未发布传感器数据或其它受保密义务约束的内容。

---

Consult the built-in technical manual and [engineering documentation](docs/README.md) first. Use Bug report for reproducible defects and Maintenance proposal for narrowly scoped compatibility, documentation, or maintenance work. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities instead of opening a public Issue.

The project does not provide commercial support, proprietary-format reverse engineering, sensor tuning services, or a general ISP solution. Clear in-scope reports are appreciated, but response and fix timelines are not guaranteed. Do not upload customer RAW files, unreleased sensor data, or other confidential material.
